Privacy Policy for BudStudy

Effective date: September 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the BudStudy mobile application ("BudStudy," "the app," "we," "us," or "our") handles information. BudStudy is designed as a local-first student productivity app. Core academic data stays on your device unless you explicitly export it.

1. Summary

  • BudStudy does not require an account, login, email address, phone number, or student number.
  • Your core academic data is stored locally on your device.
  • We do not operate a backend that receives or stores your academic planner data.
  • Your academic data is not used as custom advertising targeting data.
  • Notifications and the Today Widget may display limited academic information on your device when those features are enabled.
  • Backup export is user-initiated. You choose where the exported file is stored or shared.
  • BudStudy includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK for Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent and privacy messaging. BudStudy does not send your academic planner data as advertising targeting metadata.
  • BudStudy does not use a separate analytics SDK in the application build described by this policy.

2. Information You Create in BudStudy

Depending on how you use the app, you may create academic and planning information such as:

  • semesters and courses;
  • class timetable entries;
  • assignments and exams;
  • tasks and reminders;
  • study goals and study sessions;
  • attendance records;
  • grades and grade-related information; and
  • notification and app preferences.

This information is stored in local application storage on your device. It is not uploaded to a BudStudy or BudBud developer server.

3. No Account or Cloud Sync

BudStudy does not require a user account and does not provide automatic account-based cloud synchronization for your academic data. We do not ask you to provide an email address, phone number, or student number in order to use the core planner.

4. Notifications and Today Widget

If you enable notifications or add the BudStudy Today Widget, limited academic information may be shown by your operating system outside the main app interface. Depending on your data and settings, this may include items such as course names, assignment or exam information, reminder text, times, or summary status.

This information is used only to provide the notification or widget feature on your device. You should consider your device lock-screen, notification-preview, and home-screen privacy settings if other people can access or view your device.

5. Backup and Export

BudStudy can allow you to manually export a backup of your app data. Export is started by you, and the destination is selected through the operating system's file or sharing interface.

A backup may contain academic and personal planning data. The V1 backup format is not required to be password-protected or encrypted. You are responsible for storing exported backup files securely and for choosing where you send or save them.

If you store or share a backup through a third-party service such as a cloud drive, email provider, or file-sharing service, that provider's own privacy policy and security practices apply to the copy you place there. BudStudy does not operate those services as its backend.

6. How We Use Information

Core academic information is processed locally to provide BudStudy features such as Today, planning, assignments, exams, study tools, attendance, grades, reminders, and widgets.

We do not use course names, exam dates, grades, attendance, study time, assignment titles, task content, or assistant results as custom advertising targeting metadata.

7. Third-Party Services

BudStudy includes Google's Mobile Ads SDK and User Messaging Platform (UMP) functionality to obtain and manage advertising consent and privacy choices where required. The app may communicate with Google for this consent and privacy-messaging purpose.

BudStudy does not send course names, exam dates, grades, attendance, study time, assignment titles, task content, or assistant results to Google as custom advertising targeting metadata. The application build described by this policy does not use a separate analytics SDK.

Google's own privacy terms apply to information processed by its SDKs. If BudStudy's third-party services or data flows materially change, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

8. Data Storage and Retention

Your in-app academic content remains in local application storage until you edit or delete the relevant records, clear application data through supported app or operating-system controls, or uninstall the app, subject to normal operating-system backup and storage behavior.

Exported backup files are separate copies. Removing data from BudStudy does not automatically delete backup files that you previously saved or shared outside the app.

9. Data Security

BudStudy is designed to reduce unnecessary data exposure by keeping core academic data local and avoiding a developer-operated academic-data backend. However, no device, storage system, or sharing method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You are responsible for protecting access to your device and for securely storing any backup files that you export.

10. Your Choices

  • You control the academic information you enter into BudStudy.
  • You can edit or remove individual records using the app's available controls.
  • You can control notification permissions and notification-preview behavior through your device settings.
  • You can remove the Today Widget from your home screen at any time.
  • You choose whether to export a backup and where that exported file is stored or shared.

11. Children's Privacy

BudStudy is a student productivity application and may be used by students of different ages. BudStudy does not require a developer-hosted account and does not intentionally collect core academic planner data on developer-operated servers.

Parents or guardians of younger users should review device permissions, notification visibility, widget visibility, and any backup-sharing destinations used on the device.

12. International Data Transfers

BudStudy does not transfer your core academic planner database to a BudStudy developer server. If you choose to export or share a backup through a third-party provider, that provider may process the exported copy in other countries according to its own terms and privacy policy.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as BudStudy changes. When we do, we will revise the effective date at the top of this page. Material changes to data handling or third-party processing will be reflected in the policy applicable to the corresponding app version.
